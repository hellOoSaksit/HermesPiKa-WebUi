# Design System: HPK Console

> This file is the visual design system, recorded from the shipped console in `assets/console/`. The product and system specification lives in `docs/DESIGN.md`. The earlier "Interlocking Track Diagram" signal-box world is retired: no track diagram, no signal posts, no glowing lamps.

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Night Messenger"**

The console is a calm messenger at night. You talk to one main chat in a bubble thread; the work that chat fans out is drawn beside you in a plan panel as outlined boxes joined by thin dependency lines. The sidebar belongs to the chats: a quiet "needs you" line, a compact nav, then the chat list with sub-tasks threaded under their main chat. Ground is a plum-tinted near-black (light theme: lavender-grey paper); surfaces are soft tonal steps with hairline seams, 8 to 16px corners and no glow.

Colour is rationed by what it means. One rose-magenta accent marks what you act on: the send and primary buttons, your own bubble, hot counts, the brand tile, the live plan edge and the composer's focus. Lavender is for links, focus rings and selection of structure. Green, amber and red survive only as small state dots, avatar rings and the 2px line inside plan and claim boxes. Everything else is plum-grey.

Density is calm-operator: 14px body, 13px controls, a single sans everywhere, and mono only where you would copy-paste (commands, paths, IDs). Motion is small and gated: view cross-fades, toasts with a draining bar, a dashed flow on the live plan edge, three blinks on a waiting dot, a flash on a card you jump to.

**Key Characteristics:**
- Plum-tinted neutrals; one rose accent for action; lavender for links and focus.
- State is a dot, a ring or a 2px inner line, never a filled panel or a glow.
- Bubbles: yours right in plum `bubble-me`, the agent's left in `bubble-bot`, both capped by the thread width.
- Outlined, unfilled boxes for plan nodes and scope claims; the box border only tints when something is waiting or clashing.
- Flat surfaces; one soft float shadow for layers that really float.
- Red-line actions isolated and filled red only on hover or focus.

## Colors

A plum night with one rose voice; both themes are normative. Dark is the unprefixed set, light the `light-` set, swapped wholesale by `html[data-theme]`.

### Primary
- **Night Rose** (`accent` / `light-accent`): the primary and send buttons, the split-button, hot nav counts, the unread dot on the bell, the brand tile, the active tab and section-tab underline, the live dependency edge in the plan graph, the streaming caret, the input caret and the composer's focus border. Text on it uses `on-accent`. `accent-ink` is the brighter (dark) or deeper (light) rose for accent-coloured text.
- **Rose Wash** (`accent-wash`): the composer focus well and the light-theme text selection.

### Secondary
- **Lavender Route** (`route` / `light-route`): links, the 2px focus ring, input focus borders, hover and current borders on plan boxes, claim boxes and sub-task cards, checked choices and switches, the toast time bar. `route-wash` backs the input focus well and an expanded plan box.

### Tertiary (state)
- **Clear Green** (`clear`): running, verified evidence, passed criteria, the approve ("go") button.
- **Caution Amber** (`caution`): waiting for you, stale, reported-not-verified, questions (the clarify card), the "needs you" strip.
- **Danger Red** (`danger`): red lines, blocked or failed evidence, scope clashes, deny.
- **Idle Slate** (`idle`): queued, done, stopped; a hollow ring dot.
- **State washes** (`clear-wash`, `caution-wash`, `danger-wash`): behind tags and wiki admission verdicts only. Permit and question cards stay on `panel`: a permit keeps a red-mixed outline (red lines must stay unmistakable), a question shows its state in the amber head icon; the needs strip is neutral panel-2 with a red dot.

### Neutral
- **Plum Ground** (`ground`): the page, the thread column and plan-board cards.
- **Panel / Panel-2 / Panel-3** (`panel`, `panel-2`, `panel-3`): sidebar, plan panel and composer; inputs, buttons and wells; current rows, avatars, inline code and toasts.
- **Bubbles** (`bubble-me`, `bubble-bot`): your messages (a plum tint of the accent) and the agent's (one step above ground).
- **Hover** (`hover`): a near-transparent veil for row, nav and ghost hover.
- **Seam / Seam-strong** (`seam`, `seam-strong`): hairline borders; strong for box outlines, avatar rings at rest and the sub-task rail.
- **Track / Track-dim** (`track`, `track-dim`): completed dependency edges and arrows, input borders (track); the off switch (track-dim).
- **Text / Text-2 / Text-3**: copy, secondary copy, metadata and timestamps.

### Named Rules
**The Act-On Rule.** The rose accent marks only what you act on or what is yours (primary and send, your bubble, hot counts, the live edge, the composer). It never colours state, headings or decoration.

**The Small State Rule.** Green, amber and red appear only as dots (7 to 10px), avatar rings (1.5px), the 2px line inside plan and claim boxes, tinted tag text and tinted verdict washes. Waiting and clashing boxes keep a neutral border; the state line and dot say it. A new state maps onto these four; it never adds a hue.

**The Only-Verified-Is-Green Rule.** Evidence tags are green only when verified; reported is amber, failed and blocked are red, everything else is neutral.

## Typography

**UI Font:** the platform system sans (Segoe UI Variable Text, then the Thai faces Noto Sans Thai UI / Leelawadee UI / Thonburi, then system-ui)
**Mono Font:** Cascadia Mono (with JetBrains Mono, SF Mono, ui-monospace, Consolas)

**Character:** one quiet native sans does everything, with weight (500, 600, 650, 700) carrying hierarchy inside a tight 11 to 22px band. Mono is a tool, not a texture.

### Hierarchy
- **Headline** (600, 22px, 1.3, -0.02em): the page title, one per screen.
- **Title** (600 to 650, 16px, 1.35): the chat header title, the plan panel title, panel heads.
- **Title-sm** (600, 14px, 1.4): sub-headings and plan-board card titles.
- **Body** (400, 14px, 1.55; Thai 1.7): default copy. Bubbles run at 1.6.
- **Control** (600, 13px): buttons, nav, chat row titles, plan box titles.
- **Label** (500 to 600, 12px): metadata rows, needs strip, plan line, model pill.
- **Meta** (500, 10.5 to 11.5px): timestamps, sub-task meta, plan route and number, evidence tags.
- **Code** (mono, 11 to 12px): commands, paths, IDs, keyboard keys.

### Named Rules
**The Mono-Is-For-Copying Rule.** Mono only for commands, paths, IDs and kbd keys. Counts, times, plan numbers and route labels use the UI sans with `font-variant-numeric: tabular-nums` where they need to line up.

**The Thai Leading Rule.** When the document language is Thai, body line-height rises from 1.55 to 1.7. Never set tight leading on Thai text, and keep the Thai faces in the stack ahead of the generic fallbacks.

**The Sentence Case Rule.** Headings and labels are sentence case at normal tracking; no uppercase kickers or eyebrows above titles.

## Layout

A three-part messenger shell. A fixed 264px sidebar (`rail-w`): brand, project switch, the one-line needs strip, compact nav, the chat list filling the rest, user foot. A main column with a 48px sticky translucent topbar (`top-h`). On the chat route (`main[data-route="chat"]`) the main padding drops to zero and the page becomes a full-height grid: the thread column, plus a collapsible plan panel of clamp(320px, 30vw, 420px) on the right when open.

The thread is centred and capped at 780px (`thread-w`): the log pads to `max(24px, (100% - thread-w) / 2)` each side, and the goal bar and composer sit at `min(thread-w, 100% - 48px)`. Agent rows are a 30px avatar plus bubble; cards (sub-task, summary, question, permit, worklog) sit in the agent column with a 40px left inset. Your bubble caps at min(78%, 620px).

Other pages use 28px 32px 64px padding inside the 1440px page grid. Spacing is 4 / 8 / 12 / 16 / 24 / 32 / 48px; the thread's vertical rhythm is 14px between messages, the plan graph's 22px between rows and 10px between boxes, the claim map's 16px between areas.

Responsive: at 1100px the open plan panel becomes a fixed right sheet (min(400px, 92vw)) with the float shadow. At 860px the sidebar becomes an off-canvas drawer (min(300px, 86vw)), page padding drops to 18px 16px 48px, cards lose their inset, the composer goes nearly full width, your bubble caps at 88%, and the chat avatar and status hide. Below 640px plan boxes go full width.

## Elevation & Depth

Flat by default. Depth comes from tonal steps (ground, panel, panel-2, panel-3), the `hover` veil and hairline seams. There is no glow anywhere, in either theme.

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Float** (dark `0 24px 64px -16px rgba(0,0,0,0.7), 0 0 0 1px rgba(255,255,255,0.05)`; light `0 24px 56px -18px rgba(40,24,48,0.28), 0 0 0 1px rgba(28,23,30,0.06)`): pop menus, the project menu, toasts, the drawer, the plan sheet and the off-canvas sidebar.
- **Focus well** (`0 0 0 3px` of `route-wash`, or `accent-wash` on the composer): focused inputs, the composer, an expanded plan box.
- **Avatar ring** (`0 0 0 2px <surface>, 0 0 0 3.5px <state>`): a gap then a 1.5px state ring around chat avatars.

### Named Rules
**The No Glow Rule.** Dots, rings and lines are flat ink in both themes. A lit halo or coloured blur is a bug.

## Shapes

Soft and even: 6px for tags, kbd, inline code and small nav items; 8px for buttons, inputs, the project switch, the needs strip and command parts; 10px for plan boxes, claim boxes, chat rows, toasts, pop menus and the answered-permit line; 12px for panels, plan-board cards and sub-task cards; 14px for bubbles, the permit and question cards and pills; 16px for the composer. Avatars, dots and the model pill ends are round.

Bubbles have one tucked corner (4px) toward their speaker: top-left for the agent, top-right for you. Plan and claim boxes are hairline-outlined, unfilled, with a 2px rounded state line 6px inside the left edge, inset 10px top and bottom. Queued work is a dashed outline. Dependency lines are 1.5px with small open arrows. Sub-tasks in the sidebar hang off a 1px seam-strong rail with 10px elbows. Icons are 1.5px-stroke line drawings at 16 to 18px from one inline sprite. The brand mark is a rose 8px-radius tile with a main line and the sub-task it splits off, drawn in `on-accent`.

## Components

### Buttons
Quiet until they mean something.
- **Shape:** 8px radius, 32px tall, 13px semibold; small 26px.
- **Default:** panel-2 fill, seam border; hover panel-3 with seam-strong; press scales to 0.97.
- **Primary / Send:** rose fill with `on-accent` text; hover mixes 12% white (dark) or black (light). The send split-button joins at a 25% `on-accent` divider.
- **Go:** clear fill, approve only.
- **Danger:** red text on transparent with a half-red border; fills red only on hover or keyboard focus.
- **Ghost / Icon:** transparent, `hover` veil; the bell's unread dot is rose.
- **Keyboard hints:** a kbd chip inside the button at 70 to 75% opacity.

### Sidebar
- **Needs strip:** one 12px line on caution wash with a 35% caution border: a blinking danger dot, "N need you" in 650, the first title in text-2, a chevron that rotates to open a short list of up to six. Hidden when nothing waits.
- **Nav:** 13px text-2 rows, 16px icons, 6px radius; hover veil; current is `accent-wash` (rose marks where you are). Counts are 10.5px sans pills: neutral panel-3, warm caution-wash, hot rose.
- **Chats:** 34px avatar with a state ring (running green, waiting amber, stale faded amber, others seam), 13px title, 10.5px time that turns amber when waiting, 12px text-3 meta. Current row `accent-wash`, 10px radius; a main chat with sub-tasks drops its time label so the expand chevron has room. Sub-tasks nest under their main chat on a thin rail with a 7px state dot and 12px title.

### Chat header
Avatar with state ring, 16px/650 title (editable in place), a breadcrumb back to the main chat for sub-tasks, status, a 28px model pill with a 7px dot, and the Plan toggle (pressed is panel-3).

### Bubbles and thread cards
- **Agent bubble:** `bubble-bot`, 10px 14px, radius 4px 14px 14px 14px, left after a 30px avatar.
- **Your bubble:** `bubble-me`, 9px 14px, radius 14px 4px 14px 14px, right-aligned, no avatar.
- **Sub-task card:** a 12px-radius seam-strong outline row (title ellipsised, evidence tag and status on the right); route border on hover.
- **Summary card:** panel fill, seam border, 14px radius.
- **Question (clarify):** caution wash, 40% caution border, answer chips and a free-text line.
- **Permit:** danger wash, 45% danger border, 14px radius: warning head with the red-line tag, the command as a mono well or numbered command parts (risky parts get a red border and tag), the reason, then go / allow-for-session / isolated deny, each with its key. Focusable; 1 deny, 2 allow once, 3 allow for session. Ctrl+J jumps to the next open ask, which flashes a route ring.
- **Answered permit:** collapses to one 12px line in a seam outline: dot, verdict, ellipsised command, time; red text if denied.
- **Composer:** panel fill, seam-strong border, 16px radius, pinned under the thread at the thread width; focus turns the border 55% rose with a rose-wash well.

### Plan panel and plan graph (signature)
The right panel on panel fill: a 16px title with a one-line 12px tabular summary ("1 of 6 verified · 3 running · 1 needs you"), the goal in a panel-2 well, then the box graph and criteria. Boxes flow in rows of up to four; the left gutter carries lines that skip rows. Each box: state line, 13px title with a number, a 11.5px route line (model, effort, depth), a mono scope path (red with a warning when it overlaps), state and evidence tags, and criteria with pass/fail marks. Running is a green line; waiting an amber line and amber-tinted border; stale a faded amber line; queued dashed; the current chat's box is panel-2 with a route border. Edges: pending seam-strong, done track, live rose with a moving dash.

### Plan board and claim map
- **Plan board (Overview, Work):** one 12px-radius card per main chat on ground fill, a 650 title link and a 12px line, holding that chat's plan graph.
- **Claim map (Board):** scope areas as 13px headings over a grid of claim boxes (min 240px). Claim boxes share the plan box form; the inner line is the state, a danger or caution border tint marks a clash, and a red clash line names the other holder. Paths are 11px text-3.

### Tags
11px semibold, 6px radius; neutral tags outlined seam-strong, state tags on wash with full-colour text. Evidence tags are 10px-radius pills with a 7px dot, reading "stage · confidence".

### Wiki admission and recall
Admission verdicts are 8px seam cards tinted by verdict: keep (clear), defer (caution), refuse (danger), each with a dot. Recall chips that were cut get a caution border and a small caution note.

### Work categories
A bordered list of rows (name column 180 to 220px, then the chain); each chain step is a panel-2 8px chip with a number and a small select, joined by chevrons.

### Inputs
34px, panel-2, seam border, 8px radius; focus is a route border with a 3px route-wash well. Sidebar search and filter are 28px on panel-2 with no border.

### Motion
Easing `cubic-bezier(0.16, 1, 0.3, 1)`; 140ms for control feedback, 260ms for panels and the drawer. Screens cross-fade (160ms out, 300ms in); toasts slide in and drain a 2px route bar (3.6s, longer with undo); the live plan edge dashes (0.9s loop); waiting dots blink three times then hold; a jumped-to card flashes a route ring (0.9s); the needs chevron rotates.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep the rose accent for what you act on or what is yours: primary and send, your bubble, hot counts, the live edge, the composer focus.
- **Do** show state only as small dots, avatar rings and the 2px line inside plan and claim boxes, with washes reserved for permit, question and verdict cards.
- **Do** set commands, paths and IDs in mono, and nothing else; align counts with tabular figures in the sans.
- **Do** cap bubbles, the goal bar and the composer at `thread-w` (780px); your bubble further at min(78%, 620px).
- **Do** isolate red-line and destructive actions by space (auto margin plus 24px) and let them fill red only on hover or focus.
- **Do** keep keyboard approvals: 1 deny, 2 allow once, 3 allow for session on a focused permit; Ctrl+J to the next ask; keys only while the card itself holds focus.
- **Do** put every visible string in `i18n/en.json` and `i18n/th.json` (English default and fallback) and format dates and numbers through the active locale.
- **Do** raise line-height to 1.7 for Thai.
- **Do** keep a 2px route focus ring with 2px offset on every focusable element, including plan boxes and permit cards.
- **Do** gate every animation behind `html[data-motion="off"]` and `prefers-reduced-motion`, and check `motionOn()` before scripted motion.
- **Do** verify both themes; light uses the deeper inks (`light-accent`, `light-danger` and the rest), never the dark values.

### Don't:
- **Don't** add glow, halos or coloured blur to dots, rings, buttons or boxes, in either theme.
- **Don't** use the accent for state, headings or decoration, or a state colour as a fill for whole panels outside the permit, question and verdict cards.
- **Don't** fill plan or claim boxes by state; tint the inner line, and the border only for waiting or clashing.
- **Don't** set counts, times, plan numbers or prose in mono.
- **Don't** let a bubble or card run wider than `thread-w`.
- **Don't** place approve and deny side by side with no separation, or fill a danger button at rest.
- **Don't** add shadows to panels, rows, bubbles or boxes; the float shadow is for layers that float.
- **Don't** bring back track diagrams, signal posts or signal glyphs; state lives in the sidebar, the thread and the plan panel.
- **Don't** hard-code UI strings in views or markup.
- **Don't** add uppercase kickers or eyebrow labels above headings.
